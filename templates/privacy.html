{{ c.brand_name }} is a personal lactation tracking tool currently in active development. This page explains what happens to your data right now, in the prototype. It will be replaced with a finalized policy before any public launch.

Where your data lives today

  • Pumping sessions, supplements, and daily logs are stored on the same server that serves this page, in a SQLite database file.
  • JavaScript libraries (Tailwind, Chart.js) and sounds are served from this server, not third-party CDNs.
  • No analytics, no ad networks, no tracking pixels.
  • No user accounts exist yet — this is a single-user prototype.

What we don't do

  • We do not sell, share, or transmit your data to any third party.
  • We do not run advertising or marketing scripts.
  • We do not collect anything about you beyond what you explicitly log.

What's still being decided

We're actively evaluating a local-first architecture where your data would live entirely on your device — never reaching any server. If that's the direction we go, this policy will become much shorter: "Your data is on your phone. We can't see it."

Your rights right now

  • Export everything anytime as a CSV file from the export page.
  • Delete everything anytime by contacting the developer directly.

This is a placeholder document drafted by the developer. Before any public launch involving real users, this will be reviewed by a privacy attorney.
